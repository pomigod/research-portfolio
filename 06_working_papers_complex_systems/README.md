# Complex Systems, Behavioral Economics & Abductive Discovery (Working Paper)

**Author**: Jin Hwang (`황진`), Konyang University
**Document Status**: Unrefereed Working Paper (`working-paper`)
**Original Korean Title**: `전진의 착시와 역행적 회귀: 세대적 결손과 비가시성이 은폐한 과거 시뮬레이션의 법칙 및 조건 역추적 82대 인물·개념 총람`

## Summary

A 16-page theoretical working paper synthesizing behavioral economics, complex adaptive systems, Poincar\'e recurrence, non-ergodicity, and conditional backward-tracing abductive reasoning across 82 canonical concepts and thinkers. Accompanied by a conceptual Venn schema separating *Skill (`실력`)*, *Popularity (`대중성`)*, *Accessibility (`접근성`)*, and *Luck (`운`)*.
